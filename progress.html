Milestones

Where we've been, and where we're headed. Goals further down the line are marked as upcoming, not yet achieved.

Spring 2026

LOGROS founded

A small group of UConn students decided the campus needed a club focused on Latinx immigrant and first-gen support — not just culture, but resources, opportunities, and hands-on service.

Summer 2026

First partnerships formed

Reached out to IRIS and Big Brothers Big Sisters to set up recurring volunteer opportunities for members.

September 2026

Site launched, first meeting held

Officially registered as a UConn student organization and held our first general body meeting.

Fall 2026 — upcoming

First scholarship fundraiser: Noche Latina

Goal: raise at least $2,000 toward the LOGROS Scholarship Fund in a single event.

Spring 2027 — upcoming

First scholarship awarded

Goal: hit the $5,000 fund target and open applications to Latinx, immigrant, and first-gen high school seniors.
