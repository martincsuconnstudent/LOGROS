get involved

There's a way in, whoever you are

You don't need experience, a specific major, or to already be involved in Latinx spaces at UConn. Pick the level of commitment that fits your semester.

ways to join

Four ways to plug in

Lowest commitment

General Body Member

Come to meetings and events, no obligations attached. Great if you want to test the waters or just want to stay in the loop.

Hands-on

Volunteer Corps

Join scheduled volunteer shifts with IRIS and Big Brothers Big Sisters. We'll handle logistics — you show up and put in the time.

Ongoing

Fundraising Committee

Help plan and run the events that fund the LOGROS Scholarship — from bake sales to Noche Latina.

Leadership track

Officer / Chair

Help run the club itself: outreach, events, finance, or communications. Open as the org grows.

where we volunteer

Our community partners

Support, for LOGROS, isn't abstract — it's scheduled shifts with organizations already serving immigrant families.

IRIS

Integrated Refugee & Immigrant Services

IRIS resettles refugees and supports immigrant families across Connecticut. LOGROS members help pack welcome kits, support English conversation groups, and assist with donation drives — no prior experience needed, just reliability.

Big Brothers Big Sisters

Youth mentorship

We're building a pipeline of LOGROS members as "Bigs" for local Latinx and first-gen youth, so students can offer near-peer mentorship to kids navigating some of what our members have already been through.

Know another organization doing this work? Suggest a partnership on the contact page.
