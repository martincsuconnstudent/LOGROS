about us

Why LOGROS exists

UConn has Latinx students, immigrant students, and first-generation students in every college and every graduating class — but not always a clear place to find each other, or a clear way to give back to the community that raised them.

Our story

LOGROS started as an idea between students who kept noticing the same gap: plenty of goodwill toward Latinx and immigrant communities at UConn, but no single organization tying resources, volunteering, and fundraising together under one roof.

We founded LOGROS to be that roof — a club that treats "supporting the community" as something you can actually plan, measure, and grow semester over semester, not just a mission statement.

Who it's for

LOGROS is open to any UConn student — Latinx, immigrant, first-generation, or an ally who wants to put in real time and effort. You don't need to be Latinx to volunteer with us, and you don't need to be a member to apply lessons from our resources.

We also exist for the Latinx, immigrant, and first-gen high schoolers who will one day apply for the scholarship we're building.

the name

What LOGROS stands for

Logro is Spanish for an achievement — something you gain through effort, not luck. That's also our acronym.

L · O

Latinx Outreach

Reaching Latinx and immigrant students across campus, no matter their year, major, or how connected they already feel to the community.

G · R

Growing Resources

A living, growing set of tools — this website, our events calendar, our progress tracker — so getting involved never depends on knowing the right person.

O · S

Opportunities & Support

A scholarship fund we build ourselves, and direct volunteer hours with organizations already doing the work on the ground.

how we're run

Leadership

LOGROS is entirely student-run. As we grow, this section will list our officers and committee chairs.

Starting a chapter, committee, or want to run for an officer position? Reach out through the contact page — we're building this leadership team from the ground up.
