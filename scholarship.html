The LOGROS Scholarship Fund

A scholarship for Latinx, immigrant, and first-generation high school students — funded entirely by what LOGROS members raise, dollar by dollar.

Why a scholarship

For a lot of Latinx, immigrant, and first-generation students, the biggest barrier to college isn't ambition — it's cost, and the fact that nobody in the family has done this before. LOGROS exists partly to close that gap directly: not with advice, but with money a student can actually use.

This is the "opportunities" piece of our name, and it's the hardest one — it depends entirely on how much the club can raise.

$1,240 raised of $5,000 goal

This is our first year raising funds — every dollar brings us closer to awarding the first LOGROS Scholarship.

eligibility

Who can apply

Final criteria will be set by the Scholarship Committee before the first application cycle opens. Here's the current draft:

Background

Latinx, an immigrant, and/or the first in their family to attend a four-year college or university.

Status

High school senior planning to enroll in a two- or four-year institution the following fall.

Connecticut ties

Priority given to students attending high school in Connecticut, though this may expand as the fund grows.

Application

A short essay, one letter of recommendation, and a brief financial-need statement. No minimum GPA gate planned.

Applications are not yet open. Check back here or follow our Instagram for the announcement.

how we raise it

Where the money comes from

No single fundraiser carries the whole goal — it's built from several smaller efforts across the year.

Signature event

Noche Latina

Our ticketed fall fundraiser — food, music, and dancing, with proceeds going directly to the fund.

Recurring

Bake sales & small pop-ups

Low-effort, steady fundraisers run by the Fundraising Committee throughout the semester.

Growing

Alumni & community donations

Direct giving from UConn alumni, faculty, and local businesses who want to back the fund.

In progress

Local business sponsorships

We're reaching out to Latinx-owned and allied businesses near Storrs and Hartford for sponsorship support.

Want to give directly?

Individual donations of any size go straight into the scholarship fund. A giving link will go live here once our fund is set up through UConn's foundation — for now, reach out directly.
