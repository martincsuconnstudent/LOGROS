logro, noun — an achievement, something gained through effort

Building the resources, opportunities, and support Latinx and first-gen students need at UConn.

LOGROS is a UConn student organization for Latinx immigrants, first-generation Americans, and anyone who wants to stand with them — through campus resources, community volunteering, and a scholarship fund for the next generation.
